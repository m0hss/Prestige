## The brief

Zeven Rivieren builds its site in one morning. Suppliers booked arrival times first-come, so almost
everyone booked the first hour, and the queue backed up onto the provincial road.

## What changed

Slots are allocated by one person and published as a read-only view. Trucks that arrive early wait
in a field beside the gate instead of on the road. {{< stepref n="5" text="How the gate was rebuilt" >}}.
